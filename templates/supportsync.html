SupportSync Ticketing System

Overview

SupportSync is a comprehensive web-based IT ticketing system designed to streamline internal IT support requests. It allows users to create, track, and manage tickets efficiently, while enabling IT teams to monitor progress, assign tasks, and analyze response times for better workflow management.

Key Features

  • Submit and track support tickets in real-time.
  • Role-based access for users, technicians, and admins.
  • Automated ticket assignment and priority management.
  • Detailed reporting and analytics for support trends.
  • Email notifications and updates for ticket status changes.

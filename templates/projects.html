PulseCheck AI

A full-stack attendance system replacing hours-clocked with engagement-based Pulse Scores using AI-assisted verification and geofencing.

Next.jsTypeScriptTailwind CSSAI / LLMGeolocation APIVercel

The Problem

Traditional attendance systems rely on simple clock-ins and sick notes—both easy to falsify, tedious to process, and uninformative regarding actual work engagement.

The Approach

  • Pulse Scores: Replaced raw hours with a composite engagement metric built from attendance, participation, and consistency.
  • AI Lateness Coach: Provided constructive coaching feedback on arrival patterns instead of punitive measures.
  • Automated Document Verification: Parsed sick notes via AI to remove administrative approval bottlenecks.
  • Geofenced Check-ins: Enforced location validation to verify physical workspace presence.

My Role & Impact

Solo full-stack build—designed data models, engineered geofencing and AI verification logic, and shipped the responsive user interfaces.

Apply Smart

A Flask web application calculating South African matric APS scores and instantly matching students with qualifying university programs.

PythonFlaskWTFormsJavaScriptHTML/CSS

The Problem

Students often waste application fees and months applying to programs they don't qualify for because university admission criteria are scattered across separate platforms and unstructured documents.

The Approach

  • Dynamic APS Calculator: Automated the computation of Admission Point Scores directly from user subject marks.
  • Program Matching Engine: Cross-referenced student profiles against a database of institutional criteria.
  • Mobile-First Design: Optimized for quick access by learners applying directly from mobile devices.

My Role & Impact

Solo build—structured program schemas, engineered matching algorithms, and implemented dynamic WTForms validation flows.

Responsible AI · Data Science

Bias Audit: Financial Credit Scoring

A statistical fairness audit on a credit scoring model uncovering age-based bias under 25 and testing algorithmic mitigation strategies.

PythonJupyterScikit-learnPandasNumPySciPy

The Problem

Automated credit scoring models frequently inherit historical data biases, systematically disadvantaging specific demographics without triggering traditional model accuracy alarms.

The Approach

  • Proxy Analysis: Evaluated feature correlations to identify hidden demographic encoding.
  • Disparate Impact Ratio: Measured approval rate gaps against established regulatory benchmarks.
  • Mitigation Testing: Implemented and compared pre-processing (Reweighing) and post-processing (Threshold Moving) techniques.

My Role & Impact

Collaborative project with HerTech Hive—contributed to methodology design, executed bias detection code, and wrote analytical documentation.
